## Top product gaps, in binding priority order

1. **R3/R4 simultaneous mutating surfaces / stale-write boundary inconsistency** — outranks all UI polish; now a W5 prerequisite (W5A); corrected to explicitly include Restore Version and Industry Layout Apply (Class C), not just redundant editor forms (Class A).
2. **Mobile Bottom Navigation canonical R4 gap** — corrected 3-part technical scope (W5B).
3. **"Template" vs. "Style Pack" naming ambiguity** — decision recorded, label-level rollout (part of W5A/W5D).
4. **Pre-apply Gallery device preview / enlarge UX** — real but lower-priority polish (W5C).
5. **Inert/reserved `mega_menu` and typed `layout` family disposition** — decision recorded, documentation-level closure (part of W5A).

**Explicitly not listed as a defect**: Hero/Product View/Product Card/Badge being Design-Lab-only. This was reclassified per the binding Builder-vs-Lab decision — the merchant already has full access to these families through the already-implemented Advanced Design Lab, and keeping the Normal Builder simple is itself a product requirement, not an oversight.
